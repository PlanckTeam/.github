# FLL - Planck Team

## Who are we?
**Planck Team Italy** is the competitive robotics team from Istituto Max Planck in Villorba, Treviso — and for over a decade, we have been one of the most consistent presences on the international FLL stage, representing Italy at world championships across three continents.

## How we actually work
What makes us who we are isn't just the trophies. It's how we work. Every season, a group of around thirty students from different backgrounds and disciplines comes together not as a collection of individuals, but as a single unit with a shared goal. Engineers work alongside scientists, builders alongside programmers, and everyone contributes to both the robot and the innovative project we bring to the judges. No role is isolated. No effort goes unnoticed.

## What we build
Our innovative projects reflect this collective approach: each year we tackle a real-world challenge — from ocean monitoring systems developed in collaboration with CNR research institutes, to virtual museum platforms, to archaeological field tools built with hardware and software working in tandem. We don't stop at building something that works; we dig into the problem, consult domain experts, and push until the solution is genuinely meaningful.

## Why we compete
We win, sometimes. We lose, sometimes. But we always compete with intent — preparing rigorously, trusting each other under pressure, and showing up at every event as a team that wants to be there.  
That drive, more than any individual result, is what defines the **Planck Team**.
